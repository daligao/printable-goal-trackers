## Community share
I made four original, printer-friendly goal worksheets that can be downloaded as A4 or US Letter PDFs: a 30-day habit challenge, 2027 goals tracker, 7-day declutter challenge, and reading challenge. They are free, work without an account, and the generator is open source.

## Indie Hackers angle
I built the resource library as a deliberately small static project: structured worksheet data goes through one HTML/CSS renderer to produce PDFs and real previews. The point is to offer genuinely usable files first, then make a customization link available only for people who need it.

## dev.to angle
How a small Node + Playwright pipeline can keep A4 and US Letter printable assets reproducible: use structured content, browser-native CSS paged media, PDF metadata checks, and rendered-image review rather than maintaining separate preview and print layouts.
